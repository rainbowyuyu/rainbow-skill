# document-audit

Scans any document for stale content, contradictions, unresolved markers, and broken cross-references. Detects [PENDING], [TBD], and [UNKNOWN] markers that are already answered elsewhere in the document or conversation. Flags contradictions between sections, orphaned references, and notes left over from earlier drafts. Use after iterative editing rounds, after incorporating meeting feedback, or as a final quality gate before sharing a document.
