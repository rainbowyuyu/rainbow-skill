# sprint-progress-tracker

Creates a snapshot of where the sprint stands — planned vs actual. Surfaces risks early. Feeds data into the sprint review and next sprint planning. Use when asked to: check sprint progress, mid-sprint check, close out the sprint, what's the sprint status, or generate a progress report.
