# sprint-review-generator

Creates a sprint review document for the sprint demo. Stakeholder-facing artifact answering: What did we build? Did we hit our goal? What did we learn? What's next? Serves two audiences: stakeholders (top sections, jargon-free) and team/PM (full detail). Use when asked to: create a sprint review, prepare the sprint demo doc, write the sprint review, or summarize what we shipped.
