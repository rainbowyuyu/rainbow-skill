# customize-agent-hud

Rearrange or restyle Agent HUD, the notch or dock HUD on the Mac and its hover panel, with Agent HUD's HUD tools (get_hud, draft_hud, get_hud_screenshot, discard_hud_draft, finish_hud_edit). Use it when the person asks to change, customize, tidy, simplify, reorder, hide or show anything in Agent HUD, such as quota or balance blocks, rows, accounts, widgets (bar, ring, number, chart), panel sections, the sessions or token lists, panel width or spacing, or the resting HUD's mode (notch, dynamic, simple), edge, position, size, glow, appearance or transparency. It also covers hover timing and other app settings when the person asks for them. Use it for "make the HUD smaller", "show only Codex", "move the HUD to the left", "改一下 HUD", "刘海/动态/简约模式", and to explain what the HUD shows now or why a change was refused. Your changes are drafts, and only the person can save them in the panel.
